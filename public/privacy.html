Privacy

Privacy notice

This notice explains how GenioOne handles information submitted through the commercial edition waitlist.

What we collect

When you join the commercial edition waitlist, we collect your work email address and any name you choose to provide.

Why we use it

We use this information to manage early access and send launch updates for GenioOne’s commercial edition.

Where it is stored

Waitlist information is stored using Cloudflare services that support the GenioOne website.

Website analytics and protection

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand page views and website performance. We also keep request counters derived from network addresses to limit repeated submissions. Raw network addresses are not stored in the waitlist database.

Sharing

We do not sell waitlist information. We use service providers only when needed to operate the website and send these updates.

Your choices

You can ask to be removed by replying to any GenioOne update you receive.
